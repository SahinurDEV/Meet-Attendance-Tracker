Support

Meet Attendance Tracker (Chrome extension)

Need help, found a bug, or have a feature idea? Email infosahinur@gmail.com. We usually reply within 1–2 business days.

Quick fixes

  • The Attendance button doesn't appear in Meet: reload the Meet tab after installing or updating the extension, and check that "Show in-Meet button" is on in Settings.
  • Nobody is being tracked: open the People panel in Meet once; tracking keeps working after you close it.
  • Chat isn't saved: open Meet's chat panel at least once during the call, and make sure chat capture is enabled in Settings.
  • Speaking time looks low: speaking detection depends on Meet's on-screen indicators and is approximate.
  • Where is my data? Everything is stored locally in your browser. Use Settings → Backup to export it. See the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a bug

Please include your Chrome version, the extension version (shown in the popup), what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot if possible. Don't send attendance exports that contain other people's personal data unless needed.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Alt+Shift+M: toggle the attendance panel
  • Alt+Shift+S: save now
